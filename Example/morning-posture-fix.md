# Morning Posture Fix

## Hip Flexor Couch Stretch (3m)
- 90 seconds each side
- On the floor, never the bed

## Chest Doorway Stretch (2m)
- 60 seconds each side
- Do both high and low positions

## Cat-Cow (1m)
- 10 slow reps
- Sync with breath

## Dead Bugs (2m)
- 3 x 10 each side
- Keep lower back pressed into floor

## Glute Bridges (2m)
- 3 x 15 with pelvic tilt
- 2 second hold at top

## Wall Slides (2m)
- 3 x 10
- Full back contact with the wall
