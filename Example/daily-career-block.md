# Daily Career Block

## Review Today's Career Goal (2m)
- Mon/Wed: Portfolio case study work
- Tue/Thu: Outreach message
- Fri: Job board scan + apply

## Portfolio or Outreach Work (25m)
- Mon/Wed: Write or design case study section
- Tue/Thu: Send 1 LinkedIn/email outreach message
- Fri: Review job boards, apply to 1-2 roles

## LinkedIn Post Draft (10m)
- 1 post per week: design insight, process share, or case study teaser
- Skip if already posted this week

## AI Practice (8m)
- Use AI for one real task today
- Draft copy, research a company, document a decision

## Log Progress (5m)
- What did you move forward today?
- Any blockers or follow-ups needed?
