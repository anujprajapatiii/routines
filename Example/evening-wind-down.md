# Evening Wind-Down

## Screens to Warm Mode (1m)
- Phone on Night Shift
- Laptop on warm tone
- No new stressful information, no work emails

## Chin Tucks Lying Down (3m)
- 2 x 10 reps, hold 5 seconds each

## 90-90 Hip Stretch (2m)
- 60 seconds each side

## Hip Flexor Stretch (2m)
- Especially if heavy sitting day
- 60 seconds each side

## Reading (30m)
- Strategy or design books
- No screens

## Prep Sleep Environment (3m)
- Blackout the room completely
- Foam earplugs in
- HEPA air filter on
- Cool the room
- Phone face down or in another room

## Lights Out (1m)
- Target: 11:30 PM
- Five full 90-minute sleep cycles to 7 AM
