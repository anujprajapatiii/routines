# Pre-Event Prep

## Know the Event (3m)
- What is it about?
- Who's hosting?
- What kind of people will be there?

## Prepare Questions (3m)
- Write 2-3 genuine questions about the topic
- Not pickup lines, real curiosity

## Get Dressed (10m)
- Dress well, intentional outfit
- Clean, smell good

## Set Your Goal (2m)
- Talk to 3 new people minimum
- Add 1 person on Instagram or exchange numbers

## Mindset Reset (2m)
- Everyone is shy
- Other people are waiting for you to introduce yourself
- You don't need to be charming, just present
