# Sunday Reload

## Review the Week (5m)
- What went well?
- What got neglected?
- What's the one thing that would make the biggest difference next week?

## Body Check-In (3m)
- Did I cook at home most days?
- Posture work happening daily?
- Did I get sunlight?
- How's my sleep?

## Social Check-In (3m)
- How many new people did I meet?
- Did I go on a date or attend an event?
- Am I following up with contacts?

## Career Check-In (3m)
- Did I work on portfolio this week?
- Did I send outreach messages?
- How's the financial runway?

## Grocery List and Fridge Check (10m)
- Review what's left in the fridge
- Write grocery list for the week
- Check supplement supply

## Grocery Run (30m)
- Stock proteins, vegetables, fruits, pantry items

## Batch Cook Two Meals (45m)
- Pick 2 recipes for the week
- Cook enough for 3-4 servings each
- Store in glass containers

## Prep Breakfast Ingredients (5m)
- Portion oats
- Wash berries
- Ready for the week

## Monthly Finance Review (10m)
- Only on last Sunday of the month
- Total spending by category
- Compare to last month
- Update runway calculation

## Plan Next Week (5m)
- Block social events and workouts
- Set the one big priority for each domain
- Am I being too hard on myself or too easy?

## Call Someone You Love (15m)
- Anchor the weekend in connection
