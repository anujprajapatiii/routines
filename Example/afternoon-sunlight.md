# Afternoon Sunlight Break

## Go to Terrace (1m)
- Leave the phone behind or on airplane mode

## Sunlight and Breathing (15m)
- Walk around, look at the sky
- No scrolling
- This anchors the end of the active phase

## Posture Stack Check (2m)
- Ribs down
- Tailbone slightly tucked
- Ears over shoulders
- No forward head

## Quick Stretch (5m)
- Chest doorway stretch 30s each side
- Hip flexor stretch 30s each side
- Neck rolls both directions

## Head Back Inside (1m)
- Second wind activated
