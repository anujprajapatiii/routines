# Myofascial Release

## Foam Roll Thoracic Spine (2m)
- Lie on roller at mid-back
- Arms crossed over chest
- Roll slowly, pause on tight spots

## Foam Roll IT Band (2m)
- Side-lying, roll from hip to knee
- Go slow on each side

## Foam Roll Quads (2m)
- Face-down on roller
- Roll from hip to just above knee

## Lacrosse Ball Glute Release (3m)
- Sit on ball, cross ankle over opposite knee
- Find the knot and stay on it
- 90 seconds each side

## Lacrosse Ball Upper Trap (3m)
- Stand against wall, ball between wall and upper trap
- Roll gently, 90 seconds each side

## Plantar Fascia Roll (2m)
- Stand, roll ball under foot arch
- 60 seconds each foot

## Diaphragm Breathing (2m)
- Lie on back, knees bent
- Breathe into belly 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 6
- Releases deep front line fascia
