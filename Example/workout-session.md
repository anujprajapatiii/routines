# Workout Session

## Warm-Up Dead Bugs (2m)
- 10 each side, slow and controlled

## Warm-Up Glute Bridges (2m)
- 15 reps with 2 sec hold at top

## Warm-Up Wall Slides (2m)
- 10 reps, full back contact

## Compound Lift 1 (7m)
- Push day: push-ups or bench press 3x10-12
- Pull day: pull-ups or lat pulldowns 3x8-10

## Compound Lift 2 (7m)
- Push day: overhead press 3x10
- Pull day: deadlifts or hip hinges 3x10

## Compound Lift 3 (7m)
- Push day: rows 3x12
- Pull day: dumbbell bench 3x12

## Compound Lift 4 (7m)
- Push day: squats or lunges 3x12
- Pull day: step-ups 3x10 each side

## Core Work (4m)
- Push day: dead bugs or planks 2x30s
- Pull day: Pallof press or ab wheel 2x10

## Kegels (2m)

## Cool Down Stretch (5m)
- Focus on whatever feels tight
