# Dating App Check

## Hinge Swipe (4m)
- Swipe intentionally, not mindlessly
- Quality over quantity

## Bumble Check (3m)
- Reply to active conversations
- Swipe a few profiles

## Move Conversations Forward (3m)
- If 3-4 messages exchanged, suggest meeting
- "Want to grab coffee at [place] on [day]?"
- Don't let conversations stall in-app

## Shaadi Check (3m)
- Only Tue and Fri
- Reply to messages and introductions
- Respond within 24 hours

## Log It (2m)
- Any new matches worth pursuing?
- Any dates to schedule this week?
